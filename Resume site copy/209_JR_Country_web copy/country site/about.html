About The United Cities of America

Our Mission

The United Cities of America (UCA) was established in the wake of the Death Stranding to reunite the fragmented cities of America. Our mission is to rebuild and reconnect our nation through the Bridges network, ensuring the safety and prosperity of all citizens.

The Death Stranding

The Death Stranding was a catastrophic event that fundamentally changed our world. It brought about the appearance of BTs (Beached Things), timefall, and the separation of the living and the dead. In response, the UCA was formed to protect and serve the remaining cities of America.

Bridges Organization

Bridges is our premier organization dedicated to reconnecting America. Through the development of the chiral network and the efforts of our porters, we maintain vital connections between cities and ensure the safe delivery of essential supplies.

Our Infrastructure

The UCA maintains a network of timefall shelters, safe houses, and distribution centers across the country. Our advanced chiral technology allows for instant communication and resource sharing between connected cities.

Citizen Safety

Your safety is our top priority. We provide comprehensive training on BT detection and avoidance, timefall protection, and emergency procedures. Our network of shelters and safe houses ensures that no citizen is ever far from safety.

Important Notice Regarding B.T.s

WARNING: Beached Things (B.T.s) are dangerous entities that roam the areas between cities. These invisible creatures can only be detected using specialized equipment. If you encounter a B.T., remain calm and follow these guidelines:

    Do not make sudden movements or loud noises

    Use your Odradek scanner to detect their presence

    If a Odradek is not provided on arrival to the UCA, Contact Bridges services to request one

    Seek shelter immediately if timefall is detected in your area

    Contact Bridges emergency services if you suspect B.T. activity

    Never attempt to interact with or approach B.T.s

Remember: B.T.s are attracted to living beings and can cause voidouts if not handled properly. Always travel with proper equipment and stay within designated safe zones.

Water Safety Warning

Under NO circumstances should you drink any water without proper filtration. All water sources in the UCA are potentially contaminated with chiral matter and timefall particles. Always use Bridges-issued water filters and purification systems. Failure to comply may result in severe health complications or death.
