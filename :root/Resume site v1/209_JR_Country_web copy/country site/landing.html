Bridges Travel Information

Transportation Options

Bridges provides multiple transportation options for safe travel between UCA cities. All routes are regularly monitored and maintained for safety.

Chiral Network Train System

Status: Active

Routes:

    Capital Knot City - Central Knot City (2 hours)

    Central Knot City - Lake Knot City (3 hours)

    Lake Knot City - South Knot City (4 hours)

Features:

    Timefall-protected carriages

    BT detection systems

    Emergency shelters

    Regular maintenance checks

Bridges Bus Network

Status: Active

Routes:

    Capital Knot City - Central Knot City (4 hours)

    Central Knot City - Lake Knot City (6 hours)

    Lake Knot City - South Knot City (8 hours)

Features:

    Timefall-resistant vehicles

    Regular stops at shelters

    BT detection equipment

    Emergency supplies

Walking Routes

Status: Active

Designated Paths:

    Capital Knot City - Central Knot City (2-3 days)

    Central Knot City - Lake Knot City (3-4 days)

    Lake Knot City - South Knot City (4-5 days)

Features:

    Marked safe zones

    Regular shelter points

    BT detection markers

    Emergency supply caches

Entry Requirements

All of the following will be provided at UCA border entry points.

If you damage the equipment provided there is a fine of $20

If you break the equipment provided there is a fine of $100

All visitors must meet the following requirements to enter UCA cities.

Failure to comply may result in denied entry to the cities or penalties.

Cost of entry at UCA border entry points vary on how long you decide to stay.

Minimum price - $100

Maximum price - $500

Requirement Description Status
Chiral Network Connection Must be connected to the chiral network Mandatory
Odradek Scanner Must possess a functioning Odradek scanner Mandatory

Vehicle Registration If traveling with vehicle Optional

Important Travel Notes

    All travelers must check in at the nearest Bridges facility before departure

    Timefall shelters are available along major routes

    Emergency services can be contacted through the chiral network

    Travel during timefall is not recommended

    All routes are subject to periodic closure due to B.T. activity

Water Safety Warning

Under NO circumstances should you drink any water without proper filtration. All water sources in the UCA are potentially contaminated with chiral matter and timefall particles. Always use Bridges-issued water filters and purification systems. Failure to comply may result in severe health complications or death.
